# Envoy and Kong marks

- `envoy-icon-color.svg`: the official Envoy icon from the CNCF artwork repository,
  https://github.com/cncf/artwork/tree/main/projects/envoy/icon/color (unmodified).
- `kong-emblem-electric.svg`: the Kong emblem (no wordmark) in Kong's current electric colour (#CCFF00), from the logo
  downloader that Kong's 2026 press kit links to (https://chuca.github.io/kong-logo-downloader/), "Emblem only".
  Shown on Kong's dark brand background (#001408) in the scene.

Both are trademarks of their owners, used here to identify the products in an architecture diagram.

## GVSI

- `gvsi.png`: original raster supplied by the user (612×366).
- `gvsi.svg`: hand-traced vector recreation of that image (cloud outline and chip, #3949AB). Not an official source file.
